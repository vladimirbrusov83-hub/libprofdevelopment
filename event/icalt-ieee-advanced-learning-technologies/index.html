About this conference

The 27th IEEE ICALT, the IEEE Technical Committee on Learning Technology's annual conference on learning technologies. Its 14 tracks include Artificial Intelligence and Smart Learning Environments (AISLE) and big data and learning analytics; registration is offered both in person and online.
